What a workflow is

Something happens.
Steps follow. Someone acts.

⏰Triggera form, 7 am every day, a chat message, a new row, an email
📥Datafetch the bookings, the sheet, the inbox, an API
🧠Model / LLMpredict a risk, classify, summarise, write
📣Actiona message, a row, an email draft, a ticket

n8n draws this as boxes and arrows. Each box does one thing, and the data flows through as small JSON records.

The one rule to remember

Everyone does what
they are good at

🎯The modelpredicts: a probability, from data it learned
🧮Codecalculates: costs, cut-offs, simulations. Exactly, every time
✍️The LLMexplains and writes. Don't let it do the maths or decide alone
🧑‍💼A persondecides where a mistake would hurt someone

Before you ship it

What can go wrong?

📞False alarmthe model flags someone who was fine. Who gets bothered?
🚪Missed casethe model says fine, it wasn't. Who notices?
🌀Made-up textthe LLM sounds sure and is wrong. Give it the facts in the prompt
🔓Leakskeys in screenshots, personal data in a free API

Where a mistake hurts a person: send a draft, and let a human press send.
